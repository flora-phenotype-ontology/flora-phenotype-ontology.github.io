Flora Phenotype OntologyFLOPO

The traits of vascular plants as they are written in Floras, made computable: every term pairs a plant part from the Plant Ontology with a quality from PATO.

What FLOPO describes

Floras describe plants in a compact, conventional language: leaves ovate, acuminate at the apex; petals pink. FLOPO turns these statements into ontology classes. It was first built by text mining the Flora of Gabon, Flora Malesiana and the floras of tropical Africa, and has since been rebuilt from reviewed annotations of the same sources.

Each phenotype class has a logical definition, so a reasoner can place it in the hierarchy: a pink petal is a petal colour phenotype and, because a petal is part of a flower, also a flower phenotype.

FLOPO:0000060
petal pink
A phenotype in which a petal exhibits the quality of being pink.
Equivalent to has part some (petal and has quality some pink)

Colour values follow the ISCC-NBS colour system. Flora words such as French rose and rosâtre or English pinkish are synonyms of its pink class, each marked as an exact or approximate match.

Every release has a permanent address of the form http://purl.obolibrary.org/obo/flopo/releases/YYYY-MM-DD/flopo.owl. All releases and their notes are on the releases page.

Browse and link to terms

Term identifiers have the form http://purl.obolibrary.org/obo/FLOPO_0000060 and open in AberOWL, where you can also query FLOPO with OWL class expressions. FLOPO is also listed in the Ontology Lookup Service and is a member of the OBO Foundry library.
